About Camiguin Centric Riders Club

The Island's Trusted On-Demand Express Network for Safe Passenger Transport & Delivery Logistics

Our Mission

Camiguin Centric Riders Club (CCRC) was founded to provide transparent, safe, and dependable on-demand transport across the entire island of Camiguin. Whether you are a local heading to work in Mambajao, a family ordering groceries from Gaisano, or a tourist arriving at Benoni Port catching a sunrise boat to White Island, our riders are ready at a tap of a button.

Strict Rider Vetting & Safety Standards

Every active rider in the CCRC network undergoes rigorous screening and verification:

  • Valid Professional Driver’s License: Inspected and verified with the Land Transportation Office (LTO).
  • Police & Barangay Clearances: Verified good moral standing within their respective Camiguin municipalities.
  • Motorcycle Roadworthiness Inspection: Regular maintenance checks, working headlights/tail lights, safe tires, and road-ready brakes.
  • Safety Helmets Provided: Sanitized passenger safety helmets provided on all habal-habal trips.
  • Strict Official Island Tariff: Zero overcharging. Fares are calculated dynamically based on real road network kilometers (kalsada mismo) and official municipality rates.
